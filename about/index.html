Who We Are

Chicago's Trusted Pest Control Experts for Over 30 Years

Total Pest Control is a local, Chicago-based extermination company serving homes and businesses across Chicagoland and the suburbs. We know that even the cleanest homes attract pests, so we never judge, we just get to work. From bed bugs, termites, ants, and roaches to mice, rats, raccoons, squirrels, bees, and wasps, our trained and certified technicians eliminate the problem quickly, with minimum disruption to your daily life.

What sets us apart is how we work. We practice integrated pest management (IPM), favoring child and pet safe treatments, tamper-resistant bait stations, and non-poisonous indoor methods wherever possible. We're open seven days a week with same-day service, every estimate is free, and every job is guaranteed, with no long-term contract to sign. If the pests come back, so do we.
