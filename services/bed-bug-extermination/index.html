Sleep Easy Again, Fast

Bed bugs are among the toughest pests to eliminate. They hide in mattress seams, bed frames, baseboards, outlets, and furniture, surviving months without feeding and hitchhiking on luggage, clothing, and secondhand items. Store-bought sprays scatter them deeper into walls and make infestations worse. Professional treatment is the only reliable way to get rid of bed bugs for good.

Our bed bug experts have eliminated infestations across Chicago for over 30 years, in single-family homes, high-rise apartments, condos, hotels, and dormitories. Using advanced protocols and integrated pest management methodologies, we treat every harborage point and follow up to confirm complete elimination. Service is discreet, treatments are child and pet safe, and results are guaranteed.

What Our Bed Bug Service Includes

Thorough Inspection

Advanced Treatment Protocols

Proven, professionally applied treatments that reach bed bugs where they hide, including eggs and nymphs.

Follow-Up Visits

We return to verify complete elimination and re-treat if needed, because one missed egg can restart an infestation.
